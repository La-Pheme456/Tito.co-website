A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your
first fitting.

Have another question? Talk to us about your outfit, your occasion and what you have in mind.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
How do I start an enquiry?

Choose “Create your look” and share your idea and occasion. The form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. You can also call or email us directly.

When are my measurements taken?

Measurements are taken before production. Contact us to arrange the measurement process and discuss your preferred fit.

Can I personalise a collection look?

Tell us which look you like and what you would change. We will discuss what is possible for your design before confirming the details.

How long will my outfit take?

Production timing is agreed directly for your order. Share your event date when enquiring so we can discuss the timeline before work begins.

How are prices and payment arranged?

Request a price for your selected look. Pricing is discussed directly and may be negotiable. Confirm payment arrangements with us before placing your order.

Can I collect my outfit or have it delivered?

Both doorstep delivery and personal pickup are available. Contact us to confirm the arrangements for your location and order.
